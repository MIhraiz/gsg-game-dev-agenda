How this was built

Playlist metadata and Arabic auto-captions cover all 45 videos (about 752,000 words in the saved transcripts). Each transcript was read to record what was taught, with timestamps, and what was postponed. For agenda checks, recordings were sampled every two seconds and compared with reference thumbnails of the instructor's Google Doc. Candidate matches were inspected visually: browser pages and app-switcher overlays can produce false matches. The cards use the last verified agenda views, mark any off-screen limits, and distinguish plans from actual coverage. The new recordings are archived at the best available quality, with 480p analysis copies used for detection and full-resolution crops for reading. Captions are machine-generated; covered topics are paraphrased and timestamps are approximate. The recording total is recalculated from the current 45-entry playlist metadata.
